Cozy Mixer

Bedtime, without the battle

A sound machine for kids with a small pixel-art village inside it. You choose the sounds, the village gets ready for bed, and your child drifts off along with it.

Coming soon to the App Store

For iPhone and iPad

Every sound that settles them

100 calming sounds, and every one of them is free to use.

  • Soft Rain
  • Fireplace
  • Purring Cat
  • Soft Piano
  • Water Stream
  • Bird Sanctuary
  • Deep Jungle
  • White Noise
  • Pink Noise
  • Brown Noise
  • and 90 more

Each sound has its own volume slider, so the rain can stay soft while the fire crackles a little louder.

There are also nine ready-made mixes for naps and nights, for when you just want to press play.

A village that gets sleepy too

While your mix plays, the village goes through its evening. The sky gets dark, the villagers gather around the campfire, a fairy floats over the pond, and as it gets late they all go to sleep.

Made for bedtime

  • Keeps playing when the screen is locked
  • AirPlay to any speaker, or cast to a Chromecast Premium
  • A sleep timer that fades the sound out slowly, from 15 minutes to all night Premium
  • Bedtime schedules: pick the nights, and a reminder starts your mix with one tap Premium
  • A Home Screen widget to play, pause and set the timer without opening the app Premium

Works offline

Every sound is stored in the app, so there's nothing to download and no account to create. It works fine in airplane mode.

Ads are never personalized, and the app never asks for your advertising ID.
