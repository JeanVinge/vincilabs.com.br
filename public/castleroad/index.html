TheCastle Road

Walk a 2.2 km road by torchlight. Swipe to fight what waits in the dark, keep your torch burning, and climb to the castle.

App Store: coming soon

Coming soon to Google Play

Free to download, with ads after a death. Runs on iPhone, iPad and Android, in portrait or landscape on tablets.

What you do

  1. 01 · Walk by torchlight

    Start at the forest edge and follow the road through a valley, past an inn and a chapel, and up the switchbacks. The torch burns fuel and lights the dark, so keep it fed.

  2. 02 · Fight with swipes

    Slash, swing heavy, or thrust. Block at the right moment for a perfect block, dodge, or strike with the torch. Wolves, bandits, brutes, skeletons, parapet archers and armored knights all come for you.

  3. 03 · Face the Lantern King

    At the top waits the Lantern King, a final boss in three phases. Collect embers along the road.

Features

  • Three difficultiesPilgrim, Knight and Lantern-bearer. Pick the road that fits your nerve.
  • An offline GuideHints built into the game. It works without a connection, and your questions never leave the device.
  • Tilt aiming and cameraTilt your phone to aim and to move the camera.
  • Haptics and musicHaptic feedback on the hits, and music for the road.
  • Tablets in any orientationiPad and Android tablets play in portrait or landscape.
  • Many languagesPlay in many languages.

Castle Road Pass

  • No ads.
  • Respawn at checkpoints instead of starting over at the forest edge.
  • Buy upgrades in the Store.
  • A weekly ember gift.
  • New roads with each update.

The Pass is an auto-renewable subscription, yearly with a 3-day free trial or monthly. The price is shown in the store before you buy. Payment is charged to your Apple ID or Google account, and you can cancel any time in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings.

The game is free. After a death, a full-screen ad may play (at most one every 3 minutes). Free players restart from the forest edge and lose their embers. Terms of use
