Delete your data

2048 Memes for iOS and Android · Updated September 16, 2026

The short version

  • 2048 Memes has no account, and we don't keep any of your data on our servers.
  • Your game, best score and settings live only on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes them.
  • Want us to confirm there's nothing to delete, or have a question? Email us and we'll answer within 30 days.

How to ask for your data to be deleted

  • Send an email to contato@vincilabs.com.br with the subject "2048 Memes data deletion".
  • Tell us which device you play on (iPhone, iPad or Android). You don't need to send any account or personal details, because the app has none.
  • We'll reply within 30 days to confirm the request and explain what was deleted.

What gets deleted

  • On your device: your current game, best score and settings. Uninstalling 2048 Memes removes all of it straight away. You can also clear the app's storage in your phone's settings.
  • On our side: we don't store any data about you, so there's nothing for us to keep or delete. The emails you send us are deleted once your request is answered.

What we can't delete for you

  • Ad data held by Google: the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect device identifiers, a rough location and ad interactions to show ads. Google keeps that data under its own policies. To limit or delete it, reset or delete your advertising ID (on Android, Settings › Google › Ads; on iPhone, turn off tracking in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking), and see Google's data and privacy controls.
  • Purchase records: if you subscribed to Remove ads, Apple or Google keep the purchase record, as their own rules and the law require. You can manage or cancel the subscription in your App Store or Google Play account.

See our privacy policy for the full details.
