2048 Memes

A merge puzzle where every tile is a reaction face. Swipe to slide the tiles, and when two matching faces touch, they merge into the next face up. Each one reacts a little harder than the last, from a polite at 2 to the stone face at 2048.

Download on theApp Store

Coming soon to Google Play

Free, with ads. You don't need an account or an internet connection to play.

Every face, in order

Every time two tiles merge, the number doubles and the face changes. The number sits under the face, like the bottom text on a meme.

Get the stone face at 2048 and you win. Tap Keep going to play on, past the alien at 4096 and up to the crown at 8192.

From 64 up, the first time you make a new face, it slams onto the screen with its own caption. The skull at 1024 shows up saying OK I'M DEAD. The rest you'll find on your own.

Good to know

Plays offline
The game doesn't need an internet connection, so you can play on a flight or anywhere the signal drops.
No account
There's nothing to sign up for. Your best score and settings are saved on your phone.
Free, with ads
A banner sits below the board. Now and then, when you start a new game after a game over, a full-screen ad plays first. You'll never get one in the middle of a game.

Read the privacy policy

Where is my game saved?

On your phone. Your current board, best score and settings are stored in the app. There's no account, so we don't have a copy, and deleting the app deletes them.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

In the game, open Settings and tap Ad privacy choices. On iPhone, you can also turn off tracking in the iOS Settings app, under Privacy & Security › Tracking. The game plays the same either way.
